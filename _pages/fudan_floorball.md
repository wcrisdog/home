[Floorball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floorball) is a sport played with five players and a goalkeeper in each team. Fudan Floorball Association tries to create an inclusive platform for student engagement, as this sport unfamiliar enough to level the playing field and welcoming to all.  

We hold activities and training twice a week, along with many special events for people to try out this sport.
